# Six Chibi Characters Models

**Project:** Six Chibi Characters Models
**Runtime:** Arcana Squad (`src/game/heroes.ts`, `src/game/meshes.ts`)
**Design source:** [`six-chibi-characters-models.html`](./six-chibi-characters-models.html) — committed source viewer, Arcana Squad — Six-Path Pixel-Fantasy Tarot.

## Lineage

The six playable Circle heroes are the in-game adaptation of the design document’s six procedural low-poly chibi models. Existing hero IDs, gameplay stats, portraits, and battle interfaces remain stable. Names, familiar forms, palettes, props, and mesh accents now follow the source design.

- **The Star Ascendant** — Astral Otter Mage; violet/gold; star wand and astral orbs.
- **The Windblade Sentinel** — Feline Air Warrior; blue/gold; twin windblades and crests.
- **The Rootward Sage** — Rabbit Earth Mage; green/gold; pentacle disc and emblem.
- **The Emberhand Prophet** — Raven Fire Mage; red/gold; burning staff and flame spirit.
- **The Tidekeeper** — Turtle Water Healer; teal/gold; shell and gem cup.
- **The Worldbringer** — Dragon Cosmic Priest; white/gold; green wings and twin-orb staff.

The viewer remains the source reference for future model-sheet revisions. No external model package or dependency is required; runtime geometry stays procedural Three.js.
